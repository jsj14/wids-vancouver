About

A conference built on four ideas.

Part of a worldwide effort to put women at the center of data science, WiDS Vancouver gathers researchers, practitioners, and students to talk plainly about the work — not just present the polished version of it.

Rigor

Technical talks that show the method, the failure modes, and the uncertainty — not just the headline result.

Mentorship

Pairing students and early-career practitioners with people a few steps further down the same road.

Access

Travel grants, childcare support, and a fee structure that never gates the room by income.

Community

A local chapter of the global WiDS network, built to outlast any single conference day.

Get Involved

Three ways to be part of it.

The next edition is taking shape. Tell us how you'd like in — on stage, behind the scenes, or backing it.

Propose a talk on applied ML, statistics, data ethics, infrastructure, or anything you'd want to hear about if you were in the audience. First-time speakers welcome — we'll help you shape the talk.

We read every proposal. Expect to hear back either way within two weeks of the call closing.

Volunteers run the day — registration, rooms, speakers, photos. A few hours gets you a front-row seat and a WiDS Vancouver crew t-shirt.
